Youthful looking as elves are won't to be, Elana stands at a short five foot two with a slim and toned build (the only bit of 'fat' beyond her generally cuddly appearance are her curvy assets). She has bright emerald eyes, and fiery red hair, typically tied back in a messy bun. Background
Born in 2052 as the youngest child of the Wagner family (a moderately influencial noble family), and due to being the odd child out was almost swept under the carpet. In the Black Forest it was orks and trolls first after all, having a *dryad* of all metatypes represent part of the family. Subsequently she diverged from the interests of her siblings, guided on by a curious voice in the back of her head. Investigating technology and forms of magic outside of the seemingly standard for the Forest practice of Druidic magic.

She would later find out this curious voice was in fact Raven, her totemic mentor, and the source of many troubles in her future.

Her family was more than happy to be rid of her when she became old enough to attend college, traveling abroad and visiting several universities in her search for knowledge. Elena was more than happy to not even think about home... until she returned one day during the winter season. Raven turned his curiosity towards what was happening with her family, thus she had to find out.

They were fine, it turned out, mostly untouched (though disgruntled) at the change of the kingdom into a republic (sure their forturne was still more or less intact, but they were less than even puppet lords and ladies). So she ventured off into the city, getting drunk in a bar and guiding a few strang tourists out into the forest. This is when she met Morrigan, who was in the form of a bear at the time, the tourists were scared off but the dryad was too drunk to give a damn. She tried to tame it, and things led to another, until she made a new close friend.

Years down the line, she ends up kidnapped, with Morrigan hot on pursuit of those who took the dryad. This is how the two end up in Seattle. What happens next, is up for you to decide. Oh reader mine.
